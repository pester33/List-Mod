# GD Gaming List

Browse the [GD Gaming List](http://gdgl.x10.mx/gdgaminglist/) without leaving Geometry Dash.

Open **Search** and press **GD Gaming List**. You'll see every level in order with its rank and who published and verified it. Use the tabs to switch between **Main** and **Extended**, and press **View** to open a level and play it.

This mod only reads the list from the website. It isn't made by the list's staff.
