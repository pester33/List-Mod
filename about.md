# GD Gaming List

Browse the [GD Gaming List](http://gdgl.x10.mx/gdgaminglist/) without leaving Geometry Dash.

Open **Search** and press **GD Gaming List**. You'll see every level in order with its rank and who published and verified it. Use the tabs to switch between **Main** and **Extended**, type in the search bar to find a level, and press **View** to open a level and play it.

The mod checks for updates when GD starts and can install them for you; just restart GD afterwards.

Official mod for the GD Gaming List, made by the list staff.
